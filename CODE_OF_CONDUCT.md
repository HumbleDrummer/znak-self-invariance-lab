# Code of Conduct

ZNAK Self-Invariance Lab is an open research workspace.

Participation should preserve three things: human dignity, evidence integrity, and reproducibility.

## Expected

- Challenge claims with evidence, not people.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Mark uncertainty explicitly.
- Preserve contradictory results instead of hiding them.
- Report failed experiments as carefully as successful ones.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and third-party rights.
- Do not pressure contributors to reveal personal identity beyond what they choose to publish.

## Not acceptable

- Fabricated or altered evidence.
- Misrepresenting an inference as an executed result.
- Publishing secrets, credentials, personal data, or private prompts.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or targeted humiliation.
- Deliberately manipulating a benchmark or test while presenting the result as clean.

## Research disagreement

Disagreement is expected. A competing explanation should be recorded as a competing explanation, not suppressed because it weakens a preferred hypothesis.

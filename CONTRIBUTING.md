# Contributing

This repository is an open research notebook, not a finished safety standard.

Contributions are welcome when they are reproducible, clearly scoped, and separate observation from interpretation.

Please include:
- exact experimental condition
- model/configuration identifiers when available
- context and memory provenance
- authorized vs merely available capabilities
- expected behavior
- observed behavior
- receipts or logs
- uncertainty and alternative explanations

Do not present inferred motive, consciousness, intent, or "desire for freedom" as an observed fact.

Prefer small experiments that another person can reproduce.
